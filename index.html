How it works

  1. 1

    Depth

    Your phone’s photo goes through a small depth model that runs on the phone. It guesses shape, not size, so you mark something 1 m long and we show how sure we are.

  2. 2

    The storm

    We pull real rain for where you are from Open-Meteo: last month’s wettest day, a typical day, and the 90th-percentile day planners design for.

  3. 3

    Soaking in

    Each patch of ground soaks water at a rate set by its soil (the Green-Ampt model). Sand drinks fast, clay barely sips.

  4. 4

    Trying gardens

    An optimizer tries hundreds of shapes and spots, at least 3 m from the house and never over a septic field, and keeps the one that catches the most water.

  5. 5

    The limits

    It is guidance, not engineering. A photo can be wrong about slope; we say when. Call before you dig and check your local bylaws.
