# Image prompts

Every image is generated in ChatGPT (Plus, GPT image) in the browser and
downloaded to `art-src/gen/<name>.png`. The STYLE block below is pasted at
the top of every prompt, unchanged, so all images share one art direction.

## STYLE (top of every prompt)

> STYLE — "Wet light": a hand-painted gouache-and-watercolour storybook
> illustration with a fine risograph grain; soft painted edges, flat-ish
> layered shapes, NO black outlines, no text, no logos, no watermark.
> Gentle three-quarter elevated view. Overcast Pacific Northwest light from
> the upper left, cool blue shadows, small warm highlights. Strict palette:
> storm slate #26313C, slate #3E4A59, rain blue-grey #7D8FA3, wet asphalt
> #2B2F36, moss #5F7A4A, fern #9DB873, clay ochre #B9824F, rain teal
> #49A3A0, paper #ECE6DA, and a single warm accent marigold #E8B04A used
> sparingly. Matte, calm, quietly hopeful. Original artwork.

Cut-out requests add: "Transparent background PNG, one subject only,
centred, nothing else in frame, generous empty margin, largest size."
